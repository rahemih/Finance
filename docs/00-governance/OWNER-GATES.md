# Owner Gates

Routine engineering decisions do not require Owner interruption.

Explicit Owner approval is required for:

- First live-trading activation
- Increasing maximum risk ceilings
- Increasing maximum leverage ceilings
- Adding/changing live broker or exchange credentials
- Critical model/strategy promotion to Live
- Critical permission changes
- Emergency-policy changes
- Capital increase outside an approved band
- Resetting the global kill switch after a SEV-0 incident

All approvals must be auditable.
